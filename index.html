📘 How It Works

1

Average

Enter a score (0–100) for each subject. The Average is simply the total of the scores you've entered, divided by how many subjects have a score. Empty subjects are skipped — they don't count as 0.

2

Grade

The Grade is picked from the color-coded scale above the table (e.g. A = 90+, B = 80–89). It's based on the student's Average. You can tap any letter or number in the scale to change the cutoffs, add new tiers, or reset to default.

3

Rank

Students are ranked highest Average to lowest. If two students have the exact same Average, they share the same rank (e.g. both get 🥇), and the next student's rank skips ahead accordingly.

4

Semesters

Choose Single Semester for one term, or Both Semesters to work on 1st and 2nd term at the same time — both tables appear together on this same page. A combined Overall Result table below them shows each student's Semester 1 + Semester 2 average, final Grade, and final Rank in one place.

💡 Your data saves automatically in this browser as you type — no save button needed. Want a copy to keep or print? Tap the ⋮ menu (top right) and choose Save as PDF.

About This Tool

Student Gradebook is a simple, browser-based tool for calculating student averages, letter grades, and class rankings automatically. Add as many students and subjects as you need, enter the scores, and the results update instantly — no installation, no server, no spreadsheet required.
